# Sign-in brand assets

Google's logo is downloaded from its [official sign-in branding guide](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines), at `https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png`.

Provider button treatments follow the [Microsoft sign-in guidelines](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps) and [Kakao Login design guide](https://developers.kakao.com/docs/en/kakaologin/design-guide). X uses its monochrome X mark on black. Brand marks identify the respective authentication providers.

Google Sans is served locally under the included SIL Open Font License (`GoogleSans-OFL.txt`).
