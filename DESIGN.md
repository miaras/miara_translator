# Shiroko interface

The site uses a dark correspondence folio aesthetic across the home page, editor, and results view. Source and translation content sit in paired reading areas with clear rules and large Helvetica headings.

## Color

- Requested palette: `#5c7e8f` blue, `#a2a2a2` gray, `#d4dde2` pale blue, and `#ffffff` white.
- Dark canvas: `#10212b`. Raised surfaces: `#1a303b` and `#25414f`.
- Text is white or pale blue. Gray is used for placeholders and secondary details. A darker blue shade, `#456574`, carries small white text with stronger contrast.

## Typography and layout

- Helvetica is used for every heading, control, and body element; Arial and generic sans-serif are fallbacks.
- Large headlines have tight tracking and generous space around them. Aligned translation rows retain a clear source and target relationship.
- The desktop editor uses a wide writing area beside a preferences panel. On mobile the panels stack in reading order.

## Interaction

- Buttons, form controls, and editable translation rows have visible focus and hover states.
- Motion is limited to small hover shifts and the existing loading indicators, with reduced-motion support.
