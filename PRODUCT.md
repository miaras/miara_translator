# Shiroko Translator

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Shiroko translates text and documents while keeping original and translated sentences aligned for review and editing.

## Operating Context

People paste text or upload PDF and Word documents, choose a target language, optionally provide style instructions, and review the result sentence by sentence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The editor estimates translation cost before submission.
- Users can edit translated sentences, copy or export results, and save translations to an account.
- The existing routes are a home page, editor, and results page.
- Product copy and pricing are taken from the existing implementation; audience and positioning beyond these capabilities remain open.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Shiroko.
- The requested redesign palette is `#5C7e8f`, `#a2a2a2`, `#d4dde2`, and `#ffffff`.
- The interface uses dark mode and Helvetica throughout, as requested.

- Current product content and workflow: `index.html`, `app.html`, `result.html`, and `script.js`.
- Account and storage behavior: `server.js`.
